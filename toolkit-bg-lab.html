Backdrop for the toolkit rows

The rows have no background of their own, so the dashed ribbon runs behind the tool names. Each option below is the shipped section with one class changed, over a real length of ribbon at its real colour.

All five already carry the wider panel, the bigger logos and the bigger names.

option A — on the site now

Paper wash at 72% with a 3px blur behind it. The ribbon still reads as a shape passing under the block, but no dash lands on a word. Softest of the five.

option B — solid paper card

Fully opaque, with the site's standard shadow. The ribbon disappears behind it completely. Cleanest to read, but it is a card, which is close to the thing you rejected about the trays.

option C — notebook paper

93% paper with faint ruled lines across it and almost square corners, so it reads as a sheet of lined paper the tools are written on rather than a UI panel. Ties into the scrapbook feel of the rest of the page.

option D — full-bleed band

Edge to edge, no corners, a hairline top and bottom. The widest rows of the five, so the emptiness you noticed is gone entirely. It also cuts the ribbon in two cleanly, which reads as deliberate rather than as something in the way.

option E — no panel, glow under the words

No box at all. Instead each name and logo carries a soft paper halo, so the ribbon is pushed away from the letters but still runs freely between the rows. The most faithful to "no boxes"; the least protection if the ribbon ever moves.
